Before contacting support

  • Restart the app without clearing its storage.
  • Confirm the app version and Android version.
  • If possible, export a fresh backup before troubleshooting further.
  • Do not uninstall the app when the only copy of your notebook is on the device.

Backup and recovery

pokefields has no account or developer-operated cloud backup. A JSON file you previously exported is the recovery path for a lost, replaced, or cleared local notebook. Import validates a backup before replacing current notebook data.

What to include

For a useful bug report, include:

  • a short description of what you expected and what happened;
  • the pokefields version and Android version;
  • the screen or feature involved;
  • steps that reproduce the problem, if known.

Do not attach a notebook backup, screenshot, or other personal content unless it is necessary and you choose to share it. Remove unrelated information before sending diagnostic material.
