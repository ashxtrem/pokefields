Key Architectural Fixes for Multi-Card Screens

Bottom Action Bar

Replaced the two full-width stacked buttons (which took up ~120px height per card) with a streamlined 1-row dock: compact text button for Housemates and icon trigger for Change Home.

Avatar Strip + Tooltips

Eliminated the redundant paragraph of names ("Slowbro, Slowpoke, Goomy...") under sprites. Sprites now render in a connected avatar tray with names on hover.

Scannable Tag Chips

Replaced dot-delimited sentences (Cleanliness · Cute stuff) with neat micro-tags. Badges preserve vertical alignment even when items have 4+ tags.

Density & Area Grouping

Players can switch to Compact Mode to collapse the long item checklists into quick progress rings, perfect when checking the entire island's capacity at once.
